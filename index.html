02 // Standards & Commercial Transparency

Risk-Managed Delivery: Gates, HARA & Hybrid Contracting

Every engagement follows structured engineering phases with transparent exit gates, explicit hazard/threat analysis, and honest risk allocation between in-house engineering and external supply chains.

REGULATORY & SECURITY BASIS

Conformity & Cybersecurity by Design

  • EU Market Conformity (CE): Aligned with applicable EU legislation — Radio Equipment Directive (RED 2014/53/EU), EMC Directive (2014/30/EU), RoHS — via harmonised standards.
  • Industrial Cybersecurity: Threat Analysis & Risk Assessment (TARA) and IEC 62443-4-2 embedded security controls.
  • Environmental Ruggedness: Qualification profiles referencing MIL-STD-810H (thermal shock, vibration, ingress).
  • IP & Confidentiality: Clear contractual separation of Background IP vs. Foreground IP under NDA/MSA.
WAY OF WORK & PRICING

Phased Gates + Hybrid Contract Model

  • R&D → Gate G1 → PoC → Gate G2 → MVP → WP 1..N → Finalization (client retains stop/go authority at every gate).
  • HARA / TARA-Driven PoC: High-risk unknowns (EMI packet loss, key tamper, enclosure heat) tested on real silicon before full design.
  • Fixed Price (FP): R&D, PoC, Finalization & in-house Engineering / Architecture / Assembly.
  • Capped T&M (T&M^) & T&M: MVP/WP uncertainty & external PCB/CNC/component supply chains.
