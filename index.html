Section 01 // Streams

Three Integrated Streams + In-House Assembly

Physical products fail when firmware, hardware, and mechanics are designed in silos. One System Architect holds interfaces and trade-offs across all three streams from Day 1.

STREAM A · FIRMWARE

Deterministic & Secure FW

Layered embedded software engineered for unattended, offline-resilient defense-logistics equipment.

  • STM32U5 / NXP / nRF53 bare-metal & FreeRTOS
  • TrustZone Secure Boot & SE050 AES-256-GCM
  • SAE J1939, CAN FD, Modbus RS-485 stacks
  • 72h+ NOR flash store-and-forward & anti-tamper
STREAM B · HARDWARE

EMC & Surge-Hardened PCB

Multi-layer electronics engineered to survive generator EMI, cranking transients, and harsh surges.

  • Dedicated 2.5 kVrms isolated CAN & RS-485 PHYs
  • TVS/ESD diodes, common-mode chokes & RF cans
  • 9–36V reverse-polarity & load-dump front-end
  • Mandatory dual review: HW Peer + FW-on-HW

Section 02 // Governance

Compliance Clarity & Risk-Managed Hybrid Contracting

We separate mandatory European legislation from voluntary harmonised standards, validate high-risk unknowns via HARA/TARA before PCB fabrication, and never inflate fixed prices to hide vendor uncertainty.

REGULATORY & IP DISCIPLINE

EU Conformity, Cybersecurity & IP Boundaries

  • CE Conformity (EU Law): Radio Equipment Directive (RED 2014/53/EU), EMC Directive (2014/30/EU), and RoHS via harmonised standards.
  • Cybersecurity ≠ Safety: Separate HARA (unintended hardware/thermal faults) and TARA (intentional capture/spoofing, IEC 62443-4-2).
  • Environmental Profile: MIL-STD-810H temperature/shock/vibration testing + IEC 60529 IP67.
  • Contractual IP: Mutual NDA + explicit separation of Background IP vs. client-owned Foreground IP.
WAY OF WORK & HYBRID PRICING

Phased Exit Gates + Honest Supply Split

  • Exit Gates (G1, G2): R&D → (G1) → HARA PoC → (G2) → MVP → WP 1–3 → Finalization — client retains Proceed / Iterate / Hard Stop control.
  • Fixed Price (FP): R&D, PoC, Finalization, and all in-house Engineering / Architecture / Assembly.
  • Capped T&M (T&M^ ±25%): MVP & Working Packages executed in Jira Scrum sprints (≤ 80% capacity).
  • Pass-Through T&M: External bare PCB, CNC enclosures, Mouser/DigiKey components & accredited EMC lab.
