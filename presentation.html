05 // REFERENCE CAPABILITY ARCHITECTURE

Reference Topology: Field Asset Telemetry

End-to-End 3-Part Architecture

  • Node A (`SN-NODE`): IP67 node bolted to tactical generator or cold-chain container; reads isolated J1939/CAN + temp/vibration/tamper.
  • Encrypted Sub-GHz Link: 868 MHz LoRa/FSK with AES-256-GCM, anti-replay counters, and 72h offline flash buffering.
  • Node B (`SN-GW`): Aggregates up to 32 field nodes; isolated RS-485/Ethernet + dry-contact relay alarm.
  • Operator Console (`SN-OPS`): Offline-first workstation showing prioritized alerts (`Item 1 / 2 / 3`) and audit logs.
PHYSICAL & PROTOCOL STACK
L4 · Application: Telemetry Engine & Anomaly Rules
L3 · Middleware: FreeRTOS, SE050 AES-GCM, J1939
L2 · HAL & Drivers: STM32U5 HAL, SPI/CAN/Flash
L1 · Physical: 2.5 kVrms Iso-CAN, RF Front-End, TVS
L0 · Mechanics: IP67 CNC Aluminum + Thermal Pad

07 // WAY OF WORK & GATES

De-Risked Delivery: Phases, Exit Gates & HARA PoC

[1. R&D · 1 mo] → (G1) → [2. HARA PoC · 2.5 mo] → (G2) → [3. MVP · 1 mo] → [4. WP 1–3 · 4.5 mo] → [5. Finalization · 0.5 mo]

Why Exit Gates Protect Your Budget

  • Three valid outcomes at G1/G2: Proceed / Iterate with alternate silicon / Hard Stop before tooling spend.
  • Cone of Uncertainty: R&D & PoC narrow schedule/cost variance before committing to PCB & CNC fabrication.

HARA-Driven Proof of Concept

  • The full engineering team runs HARA/TARA together before writing production code.
  • Top risks (generator EMI packet loss, physical capture, +65°C heat) are proven on real eval silicon first.
