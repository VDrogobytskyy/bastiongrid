EU Conformity & Legal Clarity (Lecture 05)

  • Mandatory EU Law: RED 2014/53/EU (868 MHz), EMC 2014/30/EU, RoHS → CE conformity assessment.
  • Voluntary Harmonised Standards: ETSI EN 300 220, EN 61000-6-2 / 6-4 (presumption of conformity).
  • Cybersecurity & Ruggedness: IEC 62443-4-2 embedded controls + MIL-STD-810H environmental profile + IP67.
  • No Standards Confusion: ISO 26262 is automotive-only and intentionally NOT misapplied to industrial generators.
  • Pre-Sale Guardrail: Architecture & interfaces shown cleanly without giving away free detailed schematics before contract.
